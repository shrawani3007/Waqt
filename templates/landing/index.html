Academic & Algorithmic Foundations

Smallest Suitable Table. Fair Queue. Measured Waiting Time.

Waqt rejects black-box "AI" hype in favor of five deterministic, mathematically proven algorithms.

Algorithm 1 • Greedy Table Allocation

Picks the smallest available table that fits the party size. Prevents fragmentation where a party of 2 occupies an 8-seater banquet table.

Algorithm 2 • FIFO with Eligibility

Maintains strict FIFO order while selecting the earliest waiting party compatible with a vacant table capacity, eliminating floor stagnation.

Algorithm 3 • Turnover Wait Estimation & MAE

Calculates estimated wait from historical dining durations partitioned by party buckets (1-2, 3-4, 5-6, 7+) and tracks Mean Absolute Error (MAE).

Algorithm 4 • Weighted Comparison

Multi-criteria decision scoring normalized across Rating, Distance, Price, and Wait Time with transparent user-adjustable sliders.

Algorithm 5 • Haversine Distance

Straight-line great-circle distance along the Palghar–Virar coastal strip using spherical trigonometry (R = 6371.0 km), clearly differentiated from road routing.

Honoring the Palghar & Virar Coastal Heritage

For generations, the Koli and coastal fishing communities of Satpati, Arnala, Kelva, Vasai, and Dahanu have risen with the tides to bring home the finest catch of the Arabian Sea. Their culinary heritage—fragrant roasted bottle masalas, fresh coconut curries, crisp rice bhakri, and tart purple Solkadhi—is deeply tied to time and the rhythm of the ocean.

Waqt (वेळ) was created to honor that time: ensuring diners spend less time waiting in roadside queues and more time savoring the authentic coastal feast.
